Session 2: Friday 14 August 2026

Reimagining the String Quartet

An intimate evening of strings in the heart of Castlegregory: Mozart's final quintet, Kodály's folk-fired Serenade, Hannah Ishizaki's kinetic Collide, and Arensky's glowing elegy for Tchaikovsky.

VenueAisling Cottage, Strand Street, Castlegregory · Map it ↗
DateFriday, 14 August 2026
Time7:30pm

Getting home: a special bus back to Tralee has been laid on for this evening, leaving at 9:30pm. It picks up at the car park of Lynch's Supermarket, the SPAR, in Castlegregory, which is not the usual bus stop. More on buses and taxis ↗.
